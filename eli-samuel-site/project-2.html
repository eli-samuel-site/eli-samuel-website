MODIBODI

tossa de mar, spain · campaign

A summer campaign for Modibodi, shot on location in Tossa de Mar on the Costa Brava — in and around Hotel Reymar, then out through the town's beaches and back streets.

The brief was ease. Swimwear and period underwear photographed the way they're actually worn: salt water, hot stone, wet hair, an afternoon that runs long. No studio, no staging — just strong midday light and a coastline doing most of the work.
