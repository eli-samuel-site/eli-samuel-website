scrapbook
scanner

human first, digital second.

design that feels lived in. printing assets, cutting them by hand, and reassembling them into unique compositions introduces a distinctly human-made quality.

minor errors and a lack of precision make the result yours alone — it can't be perfectly recreated.

from the grain to the artifacts of multiple scans, this is permission to get messy and let go of perfection.

layer effects by scanning multiple assets, or drag images across the glass mid-scan.

pro tip: the process becomes part of the work — the marks of making are the point, not a flaw.
